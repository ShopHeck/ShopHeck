Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Fight Camp Training ("the app", "we") is an app for combat-sports athletes and coaches, available on the web and on iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data we collect & where it's stored

Fight Camp Training is local-first: by default, your data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us. This includes:

  • Your fighter profile (name, age, sport, weight class)
  • Your fight camps, workouts, sparring, weight entries, nutrition, and fight logs
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you enter one — stored on-device only and sent directly to Anthropic from your device (see below)

Accounts & cloud sync (optional)

Creating an account is optional. If you sign in (email/password or Sign in with Apple), we use Supabase to authenticate you and to back up and sync your training data across your devices. When you do, the data listed above (other than your Anthropic API key, which always stays on-device) is transmitted to and stored on our backend, protected by per-user access controls. If you link with a coach via an invite code, that coach is granted read access to your training data. Sign out to stop syncing; deleting your account removes your cloud-stored data.

Crash & performance reporting (Sentry)

We use Sentry to collect anonymous crash and performance diagnostics so we can fix bugs. This does not include your training content.

Anthropic API (AI Insights)

If you use the AI Insights feature, your training data (workouts, weight, camp details — no personally identifying information) is sent from your device directly to Anthropic's API using your own API key. Your data is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Payments & Subscriptions

On the web, subscription purchases are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your payment card details, and payments are subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy. On iOS, subscriptions are handled by Apple In-App Purchase (managed via RevenueCat) and are subject to Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies. Your subscription status — the plan and its renewal date, never card details — is stored to unlock Pro features across your devices.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Free-tier users may see banner advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalised ads. You can review and manage your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com. Ads are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Cookies

The app itself does not use cookies. Google AdSense (for free users) and Stripe may set cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
