Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Fight Camp Training ("the app", "we") is a Progressive Web App built for combat-sports athletes and coaches. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data we collect

Fight Camp Training stores all data locally on your device only using your browser's localStorage. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your personal data. Specifically:

  • Your fighter profile (name, age, sport, weight class) — stored locally
  • Your fight camps, workouts, weight entries, and nutrition logs — stored locally
  • Your subscription status — stored locally (unlocked via Stripe Payment Link redirect)
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you enter one — stored locally in your browser only and sent directly to Anthropic's API from your device

None of this data is transmitted to Fight Camp Training's servers because we have none.

Anthropic API (AI Insights)

If you use the AI Insights feature, your training data (workouts, weight, camp details — no personally identifying information) is sent from your device directly to Anthropic's API using your own API key. Your data is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Payments (Stripe)

Subscription purchases are processed by Stripe via Payment Links. When you complete a purchase, Stripe redirects you back to the app with a session token that is stored locally to unlock Pro features. We do not see or store your payment card details. Payments are subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Free-tier users may see banner advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalised ads. You can review and manage your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com. Ads are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Cookies

The app itself does not use cookies. Google AdSense (for free users) and Stripe may set cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
